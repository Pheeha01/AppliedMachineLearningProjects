# Loans and Liability Analysis 
This project explores loan and liability data to understand the financial characteristics of loan applicants and identify patterns that could support business decision-making.

The dataset provides an opportunity to investigate relationships between applicants' financial metrics, loan approval outcomes and borrower satisfaction. The aim is to understand the available data, examine the variables and identify factors that may influence loan outcomes and customer experiences.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions need to understand the financial characteristics of loan applicants to make informed lending decisions and manage risk. Without properly analysing applicant information, it can be difficult to identify patterns associated with loan approvals, rejections and borrower satisfaction.

This project uses data analysis to explore these relationships and uncover insights that could support better financial decision-making.

## Project Objectives

Explore and understand the structure of the loan and liability datasets.

Analyse the financial characteristics of loan applicants.

Investigate relationships between financial variables and loan outcomes.

Identify patterns associated with loan approval and borrower satisfaction.

Develop insights that could support financial decision-making.

## Key Business Questions

What financial characteristics are common among loan applicants?

What relationships exist between applicants' financial metrics and loan approval outcomes?

What factors may influence borrower satisfaction?

How do the financial characteristics of approved applicants differ from those of rejected applicants?

Are there identifiable patterns in the data that could help financial institutions assess lending risk?
