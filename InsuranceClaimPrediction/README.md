# Insurance Claim Prediction

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores motor insurance data to understand the factors that may influence whether a policyholder files an insurance claim within the next six months. The dataset contains information about policyholders, insurance policy tenure, vehicle characteristics and safety features.


## 2. Business Problem

Insurance companies need to assess the risk associated with policyholders to make informed decisions about pricing, risk management and policy offerings. However, identifying which policyholders are more likely to file claims can be challenging because claim behaviour may be influenced by several factors relating to the policyholder behaviour and their vehicle.

By analysing historical insurance data, this project aims to identify patterns and relationships that could help insurance companies better understand and predict potential claims.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Explore and understand the structure of the motor insurance dataset.
- Analyse policyholder, policy and vehicle characteristics.
- Investigate relationships between vehicle features and insurance claims.
- Identify patterns associated with policyholders who file claims.
- Explore the factors that may influence the likelihood of an insurance claim.
- Establish a foundation for developing a machine learning model to predict future claims.

## 4. Key Business Questions

1. What proportion of policyholders file insurance claims compared to those who do not?
2. Does the age of a vehicle have a relationship with the likelihood of filing a claim?
3. Are certain vehicle makes, models or segments associated with higher claim rates?
4. Does policy tenure influence the likelihood of a policyholder filing a claim?
5. Are there relationships between vehicle safety features and insurance claims?
6. Which policyholder and vehicle characteristics are most useful in predicting whether a claim will be filed?
